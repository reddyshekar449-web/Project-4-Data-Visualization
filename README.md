# Project 4 – Data Visualization

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw sales data into meaningful visualizations and business insights using Microsoft Excel.

## 🎯 Objective

Create appropriate charts to analyze sales performance, identify trends, and communicate key insights through data storytelling.

## 📊 Visualizations Created

* **Bar Chart:** Total Sales by Product
* **Line Chart:** Monthly Sales Trend
* **Pie Chart:** Orders by Payment Method

## 🔍 Key Insights

* Chair generated the highest total sales at **195,620.11**.
* Phone generated the lowest total sales at **151,722.39**.
* June recorded the highest monthly sales at **170,616.13**.
* September recorded the lowest monthly sales at **69,321.65**.
* Online payment was the most-used payment method with **258 orders**.

## 💡 Business Recommendation

Focus marketing on high-performing products such as Chair, strengthen sales strategies during lower-performing months, and continue promoting convenient online payment options.
